## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### "Nothing scraped"
**Solution**: Some sites block scrapers
- Try different page on same site
- Contact pages usually work better

### "No contacts found"
**Solution**: Page might not have contacts
- Try /contact or /about pages
- Look for team or directory pages

### "Slow scraping"
**Solution**: Large pages take longer
- Normal for pages with many images
- Usually still under 10 seconds
